To sync: select a clip, step to the frame where the ball leaves the hand, press R. Step on to the frame where the racket hits the ball, press C. Repeat for each clip, then press Esc and Enter. Clips glow pink on their contact frame, and the timeline shows each clip's release-to-contact time.
